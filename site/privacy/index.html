Privacy

Last updated October 2026

Songtide is a Mac app made by DCODED (dcoded.dev).

Songtide doesn't collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data. It has no analytics, advertising or tracking of its own, and DCODED receives no information about you or how you use the app.

What stays on your Mac

Your settings (such as sidebar items and playback preferences) and a cached copy of your account name and profile picture, which the app shows in its sidebar. These are stored locally in the app's container and are removed when you sign out or delete the app.

Updates

Once a day, Songtide downloads a small list of available versions from songtide.dcoded.dev to see whether there's an update. Like any web request, this reveals your IP address to the server, along with the app's version, but no information about you or your listening. We count these checks by app version only (a daily total, such as "40 checks from version 1.1.10"), to know how many copies are in use and whether updates reach them; IP addresses aren't kept. Downloads of the app from this site are counted the same way, and so is the first time Songtide opens on a Mac: it tells this site once, sending only its version number. You can turn automatic updates off in Settings › General.

Lyrics

While lyrics are showing, Songtide looks up the song's title, artist, album and length on LRCLIB, a free, open lyrics database, to find its words. Nothing else is sent, and nothing is looked up while lyrics are closed.

Last.fm and Discord (optional, off by default)

Last.fm: if you connect your Last.fm account in Settings › Integrations, Songtide sends the songs you play (title, artist, album, length and when you played them) to Last.fm to record them on your profile. You approve the connection on Last.fm's own site; Songtide keeps only the resulting session key, in your Mac's Keychain. Disconnecting removes it. Last.fm's privacy policy applies to that data.

Discord: if you turn on "Show what I'm listening to on Discord", Songtide passes the song's title, artist, artwork and timing to the Discord app on your Mac, which shows it on your profile. Discord's privacy policy applies.

Equalizer and visualizer

Both are optional. When you turn either on, macOS asks you to let Songtide capture audio. Songtide only captures its own playback, not other apps and not your microphone, and processes it in real time on your Mac to apply the equalizer or animate the visualizer. The audio is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

YouTube Music

Songtide displays the YouTube Music website, provided by Google. When you sign in and use it, Google's Privacy Policy applies to that data. Your Google sign-in happens directly with Google; Songtide never sees or stores your password.

This website

songtide.dcoded.dev uses Google Analytics to count visits and downloads and see which pages are useful. It sets cookies and receives your IP address and basic information about your browser, under Google's Privacy Policy. The Songtide app itself has no analytics.
