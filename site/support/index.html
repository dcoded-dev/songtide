Support

Email songtide@dcoded.dev with questions, bug reports or ideas. Include your macOS version and the Songtide version (Songtide › About Songtide).

Installing

Open the downloaded Songtide.dmg and drag Songtide into the Applications folder. Songtide is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens like any other app.

Updates

Songtide updates itself: it checks once a day, downloads new versions in the background and installs them the next time you quit. To check now, choose Songtide › Check for Updates. To decide for yourself when to update, turn off Update automatically in Settings › General.

Signing in

Click Sign in at the bottom of the sidebar, or use Settings › Account. You sign in directly with Google; Songtide never sees your password. To switch between your YouTube channels, click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and pick a channel.

Lyrics

Click the lyrics button in the player bar, or choose Controls › Show Lyrics (⌥⌘Y), for a floating window whose words follow the song; the button lights up while it's open, and clicking it again closes it. Click a line to jump to it, and pin the window to keep it above other apps. In the visualizer, the lyrics button shows the current line over the visuals. Lyrics come from LRCLIB, an open database, so a few songs have none or have words without timing.

Sleep timer

Click the moon in the player bar, choose Controls › Sleep Timer, or use the menu bar icon to stop after 15 minutes to 1½ hours or at the end of the current song. The music fades out gently, and the player bar shows how long is left; click it to change or cancel the timer.

Choosing speakers or AirPods

Click the output button in the player bar and pick where the sound goes: your Mac's speakers, AirPods, AirPlay speakers or displays.

Widget and Control Center

Right-click your desktop, choose Edit Widgets, search for Songtide and drag in Now Playing. It shows the song and artwork, with buttons to go back, play or pause, and skip. It works in Notification Center too. On macOS 26 Tahoe you can also add a Songtide play/pause button to Control Center. Songtide needs to be open for the buttons to work.

Shortcuts and Siri

Open the Shortcuts app and search for Songtide to play or pause, skip, like the current song, play your liked music, ask what's playing or set a sleep timer. You can also say things like "Play my liked music in Songtide" to Siri.

Song notifications

Turn on Notify when a new song starts in Settings › Playback for a notification with the song and its artwork. It only appears while Songtide isn't the app in front.

Last.fm scrobbling

In Settings › Integrations, click Connect… next to Last.fm and approve Songtide on the Last.fm page that opens in your browser. A song counts once you've heard half of it, or four minutes, as Last.fm asks. Click Disconnect to stop.

Discord status

Turn on Show what I'm listening to on Discord in Settings › Integrations. Your profile shows the song, artist and artwork while music plays. The Discord app needs to be open on your Mac, and Discord's own Share your detected activities setting (Settings › Activity Privacy) has to be on.

Some player bar buttons are missing

In a narrow window, the sleep timer, output, repeat and shuffle buttons move into the … menu in the player bar, so the song's title has room. Widen the window and they come back. In a narrow window the sidebar also steps aside to give the page room; it returns when the window is wide enough.

Long song titles scroll

A title or artist too long for the player bar scrolls gently to show the rest. To have it cut short instead, turn off Scroll long song titles in Settings › General. With Reduce Motion on in macOS, titles never scroll.

Music keeps playing after I close the window

That's on purpose, so you can keep listening from the menu bar. Turn off Keep playing when the window is closed in Settings › General to change it.

Where did the Dock icon go?

By default Songtide shows its Dock icon only while a window is open. Change this with Show Songtide in in Settings › General.

Songtide plays a music video instead of the song

Set Preferred playback to Song in Settings › Playback, or switch the track to Song on the player page.

The equalizer or visualizer can't hear the music

To shape or visualize the sound, Songtide captures its own playback, never other apps or your microphone, and nothing is recorded or saved. If you declined the permission, allow Songtide in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording, under System Audio Recording Only.

Something on the page stopped working

Songtide shows the YouTube Music website, which Google changes from time to time. Email us and we'll ship a fix in an update.
