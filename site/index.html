The Mac app YouTube Music never made.

Everything you'd expect from a Mac music app

A real player bar

Play, skip, like and shuffle from a native bar, switch speakers or AirPods, and set a sleep timer. It tidies itself up in narrow windows, and media keys and Control Center work too.

Menu bar mini player

Close the window and keep listening. The menu bar icon shows what's playing and the controls, and a notification can tell you when a new song starts.

Equalizer

Bass Boost, Vocal, Late Night and more, or shape your own 10-band curve. It only touches Songtide's sound.

Visualizer

Hundreds of visuals that dance to your music, beside the page or full screen on another display.

Your library in a sidebar

Home, Explore, your songs, albums, artists and playlists, one click away. It even reopens where you left off.

Songs, not videos

Prefer the song when there's also a music video, and switch any track to Video when you want it.

Synced lyrics

Words that follow the song in a floating window, or over the visualizer. Click a line to jump to it.

Widgets, Shortcuts and Siri

A Now Playing widget for your desktop and Notification Center, a play button for Control Center on macOS Tahoe, and Shortcuts actions: "Play my liked music", sleep timers and more.

Last.fm and Discord

Scrobble what you play to Last.fm, and show what you're listening to on your Discord profile.

Your color

Follows your Mac's accent color, or give Songtide one of its own. YouTube red becomes your color too.

In your language

Songtide speaks 36 languages, right-to-left ones like Hebrew and Arabic included, and shows YouTube Music in your Mac's language too.

Share, links and scripts

Copy or share what's playing, open YouTube links straight from your clipboard, and control Songtide from Raycast, Alfred or AppleScript.

Looks like it belongs on your Mac

Watch your music move

Hundreds of visuals that dance to whatever's playing, beside Now Playing, filling the window, or full screen on another display.

Make it sound the way you like

Pick a preset like Bass Boost, Vocal or Late Night from the player bar, or shape your own 10-band curve in Settings. The equalizer only touches Songtide's sound, never other apps.

Questions

Something else? Email songtide@dcoded.dev and the person who builds Songtide will answer.

Is it free?

Yes. Download it and use it; there's nothing to buy.

Do I need YouTube Music Premium?

No. Sign in with any Google account. Without Premium you'll hear YouTube's ads, just as you would in a browser.

How do updates work?

Automatically. Songtide checks once a day, downloads new versions in the background and installs them the next time you quit. Every update is signed, so only real Songtide updates are accepted. You can also choose Songtide › Check for Updates, or turn automatic updates off in Settings.

Why does the equalizer ask to capture audio?

To shape or visualize the sound, Songtide captures its own playback, never other apps or your microphone. Nothing is recorded or saved. macOS asks once, the first time you turn the equalizer or visualizer on.

Does it work with Last.fm and Discord?

Yes, if you want it to. Connect Last.fm in Settings › Integrations to scrobble what you play, or turn on your Discord status there to show the song on your profile. Both are off until you turn them on.

Where do the lyrics come from?

From LRCLIB, a free, open lyrics database. Most popular songs have timed lyrics that follow along; others show the words without timing.

Does Songtide see my Google password?

No. You sign in directly with Google, in the same page you'd use in a browser.

Is Songtide in my language?

Probably: Songtide comes in 36 languages and follows your Mac's language. To pick a different one for Songtide alone, add it in System Settings › General › Language & Region › Applications. YouTube Music follows your Mac's language as well; to keep your Google account's language instead, change it in Songtide's Settings › General.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac with macOS 15 Sequoia or later, Apple silicon or Intel.
