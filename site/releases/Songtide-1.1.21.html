Songtide 1.1.21

  • Share what's playing: Controls › Copy Song Link (⇧⌘C) and Share Song…, also in the menu bar icon's menu.
  • File › Open Copied Link (⇧⌘O) plays a YouTube or YouTube Music link from your clipboard.
  • Songtide opens songtide:// links and can be controlled with AppleScript, for launchers such as Raycast and Alfred.
  • Songtide is now open source, at github.com/dcoded-dev/songtide.
