Songtide 1.2.0

  • Accent color: give Songtide its own accent color in Settings › General, or keep your Mac's.
  • Songtide now speaks 36 languages, following your Mac's language, including right-to-left languages such as Hebrew and Arabic.
  • YouTube Music now appears in your Mac's language too. To keep your Google account's language instead, see Settings › General › YouTube Music language.
  • Share from the ⋯ menu in the player bar opens next to it, and Stats for nerds shows a checkmark while it's on.
